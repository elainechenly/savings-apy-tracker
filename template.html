    Notes & Methodology

    • ZIP code 98005: Used as the reference ZIP code for banks whose published savings rates or product availability may depend on location. It is a rate-comparison reference, not a claim that every bank uses ZIP-based pricing.
    • National rates: Some banks publish a national rate and do not offer a ZIP-specific rate lookup. Those rates are shown as national rates when the source supports that classification.
    • Regular customers only: Promotional, new-customer, referral, relationship, and temporary boost rates are excluded. Standard rates and eligibility terms should be verified on the bank's official website.
    • Products tracked: Chase Savings; Wells Fargo Way2Save Savings; U.S. Bank Bank Smartly Savings standard rate without a checking account; Citi Savings using the configured Everyday Benefits tier; Capital One 360 Performance Savings; and Wealthfront base APY.
    • Discover: Not listed separately in this tracker because Discover savings accounts have moved under Capital One. Confirm the current account and product terms with the provider.
    • Last Change: Calculated from the previous recorded APY that differs from the latest recorded APY for the same bank, product, and ZIP. It is not necessarily the bank's officially announced change date.
    • Data quality: The table uses the latest successfully collected value for each series. A stale value is labeled with its collection date; it should not be interpreted as a confirmed current rate.
